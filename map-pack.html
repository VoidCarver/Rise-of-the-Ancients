Rise of the Ancients

Return of Rome Community

Map Pack

Following a lot of tournament and casual games, two observations came to fruition:

- Maps were too small in 1vs1 for how agressive the game can be.
- Natural Lurable animals (Elephants/Boars) were often too hard to find, or too far.

The Community Map Pack was created to alleviate those issues.
A couple of additional small modifications also made there ways into the change list to make games more fun and balanced.

How to get the Mod:

Visit the Mod Page to Subscribe

You will need to sign in with your Steam or Xbox account.

The mod can also be found directly in-game by searching for "ROR Community Map Pack".

Install Instructions:

- Subscribing to the Mod is the only installation step

Loading a Map:

- Proceed as usual by clicking "Location" within the lobby options
- Select the new "Custom" option now available in the "Map Style" dropdown
- RotA maps will now be available for picking

Not Seeing the New Maps:

- Make sure the mod is activated in your mod list.

Version 2.2

General Changes

  • 2 Player Size changed from 120x120 to 144x144 (3 Player Size).
    (intended to better match the number of players to the map size on a larger 2P map size than the official maps).
  • Lurable animals (Elephants/Boars) now spawn closer to players (around 20 tiles).
  • Maps without specific changes noted below only use the two changes noted above.
  • Mega Random is entirely untouched. It was added to ease selection and avoid swapping to default maps.

Citadel

  • Base and surrounding forest generation changed to match the map size change.
  • There should no longer be unconnected walls/forest on the front base.
  • The back of every base should now have a lot more space to build.

Coastal

  • Map generation chances are now:
    1 Island: 80% (previously 60%).
    2 Islands: 20% (previously 40%).

Inside Out

  • Deer now always spawn inside the walls and no longer outside in the back.
    (previously 50/50% chance to spawn either inside or outside in the back).

Muddy Waters

  • Each player now starts with 2 Natural Elephants/Boars instead of 1.

Oasis

  • Changed secondary Forest size from 7 to 9 land %.

Plateaus

  • Adjusted Player Land for 2 Players on the new 2 Player map size.
    (Player Land was way too small with 2 Players on a 3 Player map-like size, resulting in Players being enclosed by wood and the rest of the desert region being a no man's land due to a missing player for generation).
  • Added additional small patches of forest (1 land %) within the desert region on the 2 Player size.
  • The map should now feel a lot more like the original 2 Player version on the default 2 Player Map Size.
  • Fixed a bug from the Official Map where you could "capture" neutral Gold/Stone mines.

Sahara

  • Changed secondary Forest size from 3 to 5 land %.
